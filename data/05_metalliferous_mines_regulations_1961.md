# The Metalliferous Mines Regulations, 1961 — Key Provisions

The Metalliferous Mines Regulations, 1961 (MMR 1961) are made under the Mines
Act, 1952 and apply to non-coal mines — that is, metalliferous and other
non-coal mines such as iron ore, bauxite, limestone, manganese, copper, gold,
zinc, chromite, and dimensional-stone and other quarries.

## Coverage
MMR 1961 regulates both opencast (surface) and underground metal mines, and
includes provisions specific to:
- Opencast workings: bench height, bench width, slope/angle of the overall pit,
  and safe deployment of heavy earth-moving machinery (HEMM) such as shovels,
  dumpers, dozers and drills.
- Underground metal mines: shafts, winding, support, ventilation and drivage.

## Statutory personnel
- Duties of the Owner, Agent and Manager are defined.
- Statutory officials include the Manager, Assistant Manager, Mining Mate (the
  equivalent of the coal-mine Sirdar), Blaster, Surveyor and Engineer, who must
  hold the competency certificate appropriate to the position granted by DGMS.

## Opencast (surface) safety
- Benches must be formed and maintained so that the sides of excavation are kept
  safe; maximum bench height is generally related to the digging equipment, and a
  safe bench width and slope angle must be maintained.
- Dump (overburden/ waste) design and stability, and safe gradients for haul
  roads, are regulated.
- Traffic rules, berms/parapets along haul roads, reversing precautions and
  operator competency for HEMM.

## Ventilation and air quality (underground)
- Adequate ventilation to supply fresh air and to dilute and remove dust, fumes
  (for example from diesel equipment and blasting) and any noxious or inflammable
  gases.
- Limits on concentrations of harmful gases and on airborne dust.

## Drilling and blasting
- Wet drilling or other dust-suppression measures to control airborne dust
  (important for silicosis prevention in metal mines).
- Rules for storage, transport, handling and use of explosives; charging,
  stemming, and firing of shots by competent blasters; precautions against
  misfires.

## Support and safety of workings
- Systematic support of underground workings; scaling/dressing of loose rock
  from the roof and sides before work to prevent falls of ground.

## Occupational health
- Dust control is emphasised because silica-bearing rock in metal mines and
  quarries can cause silicosis; periodical medical examination and dust monitoring
  are required.

## Machinery, electricity and winding
- Safe installation, operation and maintenance of machinery and electrical
  apparatus; fencing of dangerous parts; and safe winding and man-riding
  arrangements in shafts and inclines.
