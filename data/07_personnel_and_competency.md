# Statutory Personnel, Duties and Competency Certificates

## Owner, Agent and Manager
- **Owner:** The person who is the immediate proprietor or lessee of the mine; the
  owner carries ultimate responsibility for financial and material provision for
  safety.
- **Agent:** A person appointed by the owner to manage the mine on the owner's
  behalf, with authority over the manager.
- **Manager:** The person in charge of the overall management, control, supervision
  and direction of the mine. Every mine must have a qualified Manager holding the
  appropriate Manager's Certificate of Competency granted by DGMS. The Manager is
  responsible for day-to-day safety and compliance.

## Statutory officials (examples)
- **Assistant Manager:** Assists the Manager; holds the required certificate.
- **Overman (coal):** A supervisory official who inspects districts, supervises
  work and makes statutory inspections; holds an Overman's certificate.
- **Sirdar / Mining Mate:** First-line supervisor in charge of a district or
  working place; responsible for inspecting the place, testing for gas (in coal
  mines) and ensuring support and safe working; holds a Sirdar's/Mining Mate's
  certificate.
- **Shotfirer / Blaster:** Competent person authorised to carry out blasting
  (shotfiring) using explosives; holds a shotfirer's/blaster's permit.
- **Surveyor:** Prepares and maintains mine plans and sections; holds a Surveyor's
  certificate of competency.
- **Winding Engine Driver:** Operates man-winding engines in shafts; holds a
  winding engine driver's certificate and is medically fit.
- **Gas-testing:** Persons testing for inflammable gas must hold a gas-testing
  certificate.

## Competency certificates granted by DGMS
DGMS conducts examinations and grants statutory certificates of competency.
Common certificates include:
- First Class / Second Class Manager's Certificate of Competency (Coal and
  Metalliferous).
- Overman, Sirdar / Mining Mate, Surveyor, Shotfirer / Blaster, Winding Engine
  Driver, Gas Testing certificates.

A person must generally satisfy eligibility conditions — such as prescribed
qualifications, practical mining experience and (for some) first-aid and
gas-testing certificates — before being permitted to appear for the examination.
Only certificate holders may be appointed to the corresponding statutory posts.

## Chain of responsibility
Safety duties cascade from Owner → Agent → Manager → subordinate officials
(Assistant Manager, Overman, Sirdar/Mining Mate) → workers. Each level has
defined statutory duties, and failure to discharge them can attract penalties
under the Mines Act, 1952.
